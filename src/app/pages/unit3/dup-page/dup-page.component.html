Unit 3: CharaChorder Special Keys

Chapter 1: DUP

DUP (short for "duplicate") repeats your last input. In plain character entry, that means pressing DUP right after a letter types that same letter again — a quick way to produce a double letter, like the "tt" in "letter", without pressing the same switch twice. In chorded entry, DUP can be folded into a chord to help produce words that repeat a letter.
