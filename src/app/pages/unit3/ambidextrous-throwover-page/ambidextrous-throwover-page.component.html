Unit 3: CharaChorder Special Keys

Chapter 2: Ambidextrous Throwover

Ambidextrous Throwover — also called Mirror Mode — is an entry mode built for one-handed typing. While it's held, characters that would normally come from your other hand get mirrored onto the hand activating it, so that single hand can reach the full character set on its own.

There's a switch for it on each hand, so it works no matter which hand you have free. Unlike DUP, activating it doesn't produce its own keystroke to detect, so there's no exercise here — just the two switch positions to know.

Try it below: switch between the default layout and holding each AT switch, and see how the alphabet moves onto the hand holding it — same finger, same north/south tilt, but east and west swap, since it's a literal left-right mirror of the other hand's letters.

This mirroring isn't limited to letters — every switch on the other hand, including punctuation, numbers, and other actions, gets mirrored over the same way. The diagram below only shows letters to keep things simple.
