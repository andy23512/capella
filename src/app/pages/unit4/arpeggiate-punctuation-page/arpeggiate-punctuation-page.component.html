Unit 4: Basic Chording

Chapter 4: Arpeggiate Punctuation

"Arpeggiate" describes a timing, not a switch: a quick, single press and release — one switch at a time, right after a chord — instead of holding it down together with the chord. It's the same idea as an arpeggio in music: notes played in quick succession instead of struck all at once.

Punctuation is a common target for this. Instead of building "know it." as one big chord including the period, you'd chord "know it" and then quickly tap the period switch right after — arpeggiating it onto the end of the chord rather than folding it into the chord itself.

The benefit is speed and flexibility: you don't need every punctuation variation pre-trained into every chord. One chord plus a fast follow-up tap covers "know it." and "know it," and "know it?" without training three separate chords.
