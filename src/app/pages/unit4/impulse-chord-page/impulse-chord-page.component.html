Unit 4: Basic Chording

Chapter 2: Impulse Chord

Training a chord normally means opening your device's software and assigning switches to an output ahead of time. An impulse chord skips that: it's a chord you create on the spot, right where you're typing, through the device's built-in GTM (Generative Text Menu) — no separate app required.

Like a simple chord's word association, exactly how you invoke the GTM and build an impulse chord isn't tied to one fixed switch on the default layout — it's something you set up yourself, so there's no diagram to show here.

What it's for: capturing a one-off chord the moment you notice you're about to retype the same phrase again, without breaking your flow to go train it properly first.
