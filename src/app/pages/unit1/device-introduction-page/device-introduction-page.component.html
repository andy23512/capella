Chapter 1: Device introduction

CharaChorder makes a family of "3D input devices." Instead of keys that only go down, every switch on these devices can also tilt north, east, south, or west — so a single switch can produce up to five different inputs depending on which way you move it, and typing whole chords becomes a matter of moving several fingers at once rather than pressing keys in sequence. Four devices currently share this idea, each with a different form factor and switch count.
