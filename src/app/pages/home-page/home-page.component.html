Capella

An unofficial tutorial site for CharaChorder 3D input devices. Not affiliated, associated, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with CharaChorder.

CharaChorder devices swap a traditional keyboard's flat grid of keys for switches that move in five directions, letting one finger produce many different characters — and letting whole words come out from a single chord of switches pressed together. Capella walks through how the hardware works and how to actually use it, one short chapter at a time.
