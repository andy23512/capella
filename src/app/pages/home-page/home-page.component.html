Capella

An unofficial tutorial site for CharaChorder 3D input devices. Not affiliated, associated, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with CharaChorder.

CharaChorder 3D input devices swap a traditional keyboard's flat grid of keys for switches that move in five directions, letting one finger produce many different characters — and letting whole words come out from a single chord of switches pressed together. Capella walks through how the hardware works and how to actually use it, one short chapter at a time.

This tutorial assumes:

  1. Your operating system's keyboard layout is set to QWERTY - US. Key labels and positions shown throughout the site are based on QWERTY - US; if your OS uses a different layout, some labels may not match what you see on your device.
  2. Your CharaChorder 3D input device is using its default layout and default chord library. If you have customized either one, some exercises and chords may not behave as described.
